About Me

From Zero to Fully Running

End-to-end Shopify expertise with operational discipline

I build stores that sell and manage them so they keep selling.

I set up Shopify stores from scratch: theme configuration, product listing, collection structure, page building, and store customization. Once the store is live, I take care of ongoing management, quality control, customer support, and content updates — so the owner can focus on growing the business instead of running it.

My background in quality monitoring, multilingual customer care, and IT project management means I bring real operational discipline to every store I work on. I've supported French and English-speaking clients professionally, which makes me especially valuable for stores targeting European or international markets.

I approach every project with the same attention to detail I built across years of professional experience.
